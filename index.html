About

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy and Management at the University of Washington's Evans School of Public Policy & Governance.

My research examines environmental governance, with a focus on environmental justice, collaborative governance, and the management of estuaries and coastal systems.

My advisors are Cory Struthers, Craig Thomas, and Tom Koontz.
