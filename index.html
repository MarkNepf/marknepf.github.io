About

I am a PhD candidate in public policy studying environmental governance, with a focus on environmental justice, collaborative governance, and estuary and coastal management.

My research examines how communities, governments, and other stakeholders work together to address complex environmental challenges.

Research

Environmental Justice

Understanding how environmental policies and governance processes shape outcomes across communities.

Collaborative Governance

Studying how institutions and stakeholders collaborate to manage shared environmental resources.

Estuary & Coastal Management

Examining governance and policy challenges surrounding estuaries, coastal systems, and environmental change.
